The 4 Pillars of Secure & Effective AI Adoption

1) Governance & Compliance

  • AI policy, roles & responsibilities; registry for applications and data flows.
  • Consider EU AI Act/GDPR early (risk classes, DPIA, security measures, data processing agreements).
  • Logging, versioning, and clear approval processes.

2) Security & Risk Management

  • Protection against data leakage & prompt leaks; provider/model evaluation.
  • Red teaming/abuse testing, incident response, audits & monitoring.

3) Value & Processes

  • Prioritization framework (impact/effort, maturity/risk), use case backlog and quality gates.
  • Operating model: Pilot → Rollout → Operations, with metrics & evaluation.

4) Enablement & Culture

  • Role profiles & training paths, onboarding packages and communication kit.
  • Embed ethics & guardrails into everyday practice.
