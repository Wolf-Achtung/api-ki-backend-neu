AI Glossary – Key Terms

AI / Artificial Intelligence

Systems that simulate human cognitive functions such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. Includes machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI.

LLM (Large Language Model)

AI models trained on vast text data to understand and generate human-like text. Examples: GPT-4, Claude, Llama.

Prompt

The input or instruction given to an AI system. Good prompts are specific, provide context, and clearly state the desired output.

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Technique that enhances AI responses by retrieving relevant information from external knowledge bases before generating output.

Fine-Tuning

Customizing a pre-trained AI model with specific data to improve performance for particular tasks or domains.

Hallucination

When AI generates plausible-sounding but factually incorrect or fabricated information. Requires human verification.

Token

The basic unit of text processing in LLMs. Roughly 1 token = 4 characters or 0.75 words in English. Determines pricing and context limits.

API (Application Programming Interface)

Interface that allows software applications to communicate with AI services programmatically.

GDPR / Data Protection

General Data Protection Regulation – EU law governing personal data handling. Critical for AI implementations processing personal information.

EU AI Act

European regulation establishing rules for AI systems based on risk levels. Defines prohibited practices, high-risk requirements, and transparency obligations.

Human-in-the-Loop

Design pattern ensuring human oversight and intervention capability in AI decision-making processes.

DPA (Data Processing Agreement)

Contract required when third-party AI providers process personal data on your behalf. Essential for GDPR compliance.
