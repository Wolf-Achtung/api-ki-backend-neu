AI Creative Tools Overview

Text Generation

  • ChatGPT / GPT-4: General-purpose text, code, analysis. $20/month for Plus.
  • Claude: Long-form content, analysis, coding. Strong on nuance.
  • Jasper: Marketing-focused content generation. From $39/month.
  • Copy.ai: Short-form marketing copy, social posts. Free tier available.

Image Generation

  • Midjourney: High-quality artistic images. $10-60/month via Discord.
  • DALL-E 3: Integrated with ChatGPT, good for concepts. Pay-per-use.
  • Stable Diffusion: Open-source, customizable. Free locally, cloud options vary.
  • Canva Magic Studio: Business graphics with AI. Included in Canva Pro ($12/month).

Video & Audio

  • Descript: Video/audio editing with AI. Transcription, voice cloning. From $12/month.
  • Runway: AI video generation and editing. From $12/month.
  • ElevenLabs: Voice synthesis and cloning. From $5/month.
  • Adobe Podcast: Audio enhancement. Free with Adobe account.

Automation & Workflow

  • Make.com: Visual workflow automation with AI integrations. From $9/month.
  • Zapier: Connect apps with AI actions. From $19/month.
  • n8n: Open-source automation. Self-hosted free, cloud from $20/month.

Research & Analysis

  • Perplexity: AI-powered research with citations. Free tier, Pro $20/month.
  • Consensus: Scientific paper analysis. Free tier available.
  • NotebookLM: Document analysis by Google. Free.

Selection Criteria

  • Data privacy: Where is data processed? EU hosting available?
  • Integration: APIs, export formats, existing tool compatibility.
  • Pricing: Per-seat vs. usage-based, scalability.
  • Output quality: Test with your actual use cases.
  • Support: Documentation, community, response times.
