Legal Pitfalls – Compact Checklist

  1. Clarify risk classes & obligations (EU AI Act) early – "Compliance by Design".
  2. Maintain inventory/registry for all AI use cases & obligations.
  3. Define standardized selection/testing/approval processes.
  4. Contracts & rights: Information obligations, audit, security, usage/output rights.
  5. Mind IP/copyright for data, models, and outputs (input/output checks).
  6. AI literacy: Mandatory training & documented refreshers.
  7. Change control & re-classification (fine-tuning, purpose change, agents), human-in-the-loop & kill switch.
  8. Legal landscape beyond AI Act (e.g., product liability, CRA, DSA, Data Act).
  9. Establish technology radar, monitoring & re-tests (drift/bias).
  10. Plan before go-live – steer roadmap toward deadlines (e.g., 2027).
