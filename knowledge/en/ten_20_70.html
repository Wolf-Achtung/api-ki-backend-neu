The 10-20-70 Formula for AI Transformation

10% Algorithms & Tools · 20% Technology & Data · 70% People & Processes.

In practice, this means: Identify stakeholders (influence/interest), inform and involve them appropriately. This creates real adoption instead of shadow IT.

Stakeholder Matrix (Interest × Influence)

  • Low influence × high interest: Actively inform & involve.
  • High influence × low interest: Actively inform & keep satisfied.
  • High influence × high interest: Key actors – closely involve.
