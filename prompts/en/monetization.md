<section class="section monetization">
  <h2>Monetization: 3 Pricing Models for AI Services</h2>

  <div class="pricing-models">
    <div class="model">
      <h4>1. Productized Service Light (Fixed Price)</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Target:</strong> Clients with clearly defined needs</li>
        <li><strong>Time:</strong> 2–8 hours per engagement</li>
        <li><strong>Price Range:</strong> Low to medium segment</li>
        <li><strong>Deliverable:</strong> Standardized output (e.g., prompt set, mini-audit)</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="model">
      <h4>2. Retainer Model (Monthly)</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Target:</strong> Regular clients with ongoing needs</li>
        <li><strong>Time:</strong> 4–20 hours per month</li>
        <li><strong>Price Range:</strong> Medium to higher segment</li>
        <li><strong>Deliverable:</strong> Continuous support, updates, optimizations</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="model">
      <h4>3. Workshop + Setup (One-time + Follow-Up)</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Target:</strong> Teams/SMEs with onboarding needs</li>
        <li><strong>Time:</strong> 1-day workshop + 2–4h follow-up</li>
        <li><strong>Price Range:</strong> Medium to higher segment</li>
        <li><strong>Deliverable:</strong> Team enablement + documented setup</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p class="small muted">
    Model choice depends on your capacity and target audience.
    Combinations (e.g., Workshop → Retainer) increase Customer Lifetime Value.
  </p>
</section>
