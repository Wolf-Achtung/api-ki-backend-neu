  <p><strong>Phase 1 (0–30 Days): Foundation</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Goal:</strong> Define a measurable setup milestone that ensures the core infrastructure and templates for the selected AI use case are prepared.</li>
    <li><strong>Implementation:</strong> Document and prioritise the existing steps from the 90‑day roadmap, focusing on the most impactful activities, such as establishing a template library and assigning roles.</li>
    <li><strong>Success Criterion:</strong> Achieve an initial template library with at least three reusable modules and have clear ownership defined.</li>
    <li><strong>Stop Rule:</strong> If no measurable time savings appear after two weeks, simplify or pause.</li>
  </ul>

  <p><strong>Phase 2 (31–60 Days): Piloting</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Goal:</strong> Validate the prepared templates and processes through real-world pilot projects in the primary business area.</li>
    <li><strong>Implementation:</strong> Execute the pilot using the defined templates, measure time savings and quality, and gather structured feedback for iterative improvements.</li>
    <li><strong>Success Criterion:</strong> Demonstrated time savings of at least 30% and quality improvement in the pilot area, supported by documented evidence.</li>
    <li><strong>Stop Rule:</strong> If quality deviations exceed 20% or adoption fails to materialise, revert to manual review or pause.</li>
  </ul>

  <p><strong>Phase 3 (61–90 Days): Decision</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Goal:</strong> Decide on scaling the AI implementation based on pilot results and alignment with strategic objectives.</li>
    <li><strong>Implementation:</strong> Assess pilot outcomes against KPIs, consult stakeholders on governance and compliance readiness, and define a high-level scaling plan or exit approach.</li>
    <li><strong>Success Criterion:</strong> A documented decision to scale, modify or discontinue the programme, including resource allocation and timeline.</li>
    <li><strong>Stop Rule:</strong> Scale only if the pilot delivers sustainable value without breaching guardrails; otherwise recommend not to scale.</li>
  </ul>
