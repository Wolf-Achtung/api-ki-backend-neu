Note on ROI Methodology — Difference from the AI Readiness Report

This strategy report compares the gross annual savings against the total investment over 12 months (including software, implementation, training, and coordination). The AI Readiness Report additionally deducts the ongoing tool costs (OPEX) from the annual benefit, thereby reporting the more conservative net return. The differing ROI and break-even figures result from methodology, they are not contradictory — both calculation approaches are standard industry practice.
