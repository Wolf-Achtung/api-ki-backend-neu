Creative Tools {{report_year}} – Modular Alternatives

Many creative tasks can now be done efficiently with a modular toolset – often cheaper than im monolithic subscription ecosystem. The following have proven useful in practice:

  • Figma (UI/UX, Collaboration): Real-time prototyping & teamwork in browser.
  • Affinity 3 (Graphics/Layout): Vector & raster without subscription – strong alternative for classic graphics.
  • DaVinci Resolve (Video/Grading): Professional editing, color correction and effects; many features free – often sufficient instead of Premiere.
  • Blender (3D/VFX): Open-source suite for 3D modeling, animation & rendering.
  • Milanote (Concept/Moodboards): Visual thinking & idea organization in early project phases.

Conclusion: Adobe remains relevant for special cases. For many SMEs, the above tools cover most creative daily needs completely.
